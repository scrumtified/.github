# Scrumtify

Modern ERP software for businesses that want one system for finance, operations, sales, inventory, HR, and customer service.

We help teams run faster with a connected platform that brings the whole business into one place. From growing companies to multi-branch organizations, Scrumtify is built to simplify daily operations, improve visibility, and support better decisions.

## What We Build

Scrumtify delivers a practical ERP suite designed to cover the core workflows businesses rely on every day:

- Finance and accounting
- Procurement and purchasing
- Inventory and warehouse management
- Sales and invoicing
- CRM and customer follow-up
- HR and employee administration
- Reporting and business dashboards

## Why Teams Choose Scrumtify

- One platform instead of disconnected tools
- Faster operations with fewer manual steps
- Clear reporting for leaders and finance teams
- Flexible enough for different business models
- Built to support growth across multiple locations

## Designed For

Scrumtify is a strong fit for organizations in:

- Retail and distribution

- Professional services
- Trading and wholesale
- Multi-branch operations

## How We Compare

If you are familiar with ERP platforms like Mekari, Majoo, or ESB, Scrumtify is positioned for the same kind of business need: a reliable all-in-one system that helps teams manage operations more efficiently.

The difference is in the experience we want to deliver: straightforward workflows, practical implementation, and a product that feels built around real business operations.
